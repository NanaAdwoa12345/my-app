Project 04 Recruitment & Flyer

Restaurant Hiring Flyer

MD's Delight Bar and Grill

A recruitment flyer designed in Canva for MD's Delight Bar and Grill. The layout pairs a warm, editorial bar photo with a clean text panel — bold callout for the headline, a simple list of open positions (Bar Tender, Sales Boy or Girl, Cleaner, Driver), and clear call-to-action buttons for applying and getting in touch.

Design Execution:
  • Editorial split layout pairing ambiance photography with structured copy
  • Prominent cursive accent typography ("Hiring!") for maximum catch
  • Action-oriented CTA button and contact badge
